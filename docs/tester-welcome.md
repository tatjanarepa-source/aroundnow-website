Hi! 👋

I'm building **Wigglebi**, a map of what's on for kids near you: baby and toddler groups, classes, clubs, rhyme times,
swimming, soft play and family events, all in one place. It covers London for now.

Would you try it for a week and tell me what you think? 🙏

👉 **wigglebi.com** (works in your phone's browser; you can "Add to Home Screen" so it opens like an app)

Things to try:
1. Search your area (or tap "Near me") and pick your child's age
2. Tap a day to see what's on, or use Filters (free, swimming, dance, special needs friendly…)
3. Open a class you know: are the times and price right?
4. Missing a class you go to? Tap **+** to add it, or take a photo of a poster and it fills in for you
5. Something wrong? Tap **"Something wrong with this class? Tell us"** on the class, or **"Tell us"** at the bottom

Anything confusing, broken or missing, just tell me. Small things too! Every message comes straight to me.

Thank you! 💛
[Tatjana]
